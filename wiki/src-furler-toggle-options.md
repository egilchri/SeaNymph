# Jib Furler Drum Toggle Options

**Type:** personal-notes (AI research)
**Author/Origin:** Edgar Gilchrist / AI research session
**URL or file:** raw/Jib Furler Drum Toggle Options.md

## Summary

AI research into toggle and link options for the bottom of a jib furler drum on a Cape Dory 25D. The research assumed a **Schaefer 1100/2100 series** furler, which is **not correct for SeaNymph** — the furler is confirmed Harken. The Schaefer part numbers and prices documented here do not apply. See [[furler]] for the correct Harken parts.

The source is useful for understanding the toggle/link concept: the integral toggle connects the furler drum base to the headstay and chainplate, providing universal motion to prevent fatigue cracking. A "long" or "extension" version raises the drum for anchor/bow roller clearance.

## Key Takeaways

- **Not applicable to SeaNymph** — Schaefer parts only; SeaNymph has a Harken furler
- Toggle/link function: connects drum base to chainplate; allows universal motion to prevent rig fatigue
- Standard vs. long: if the bow anchor hits the drum, the long/extended version creates clearance — measure drum-to-deck distance before ordering
- General price range for this class of part: standard integral toggle $310–345; long/extended $370–445; simpler double jaw toggle $144–160 (requires separate link tang)
- For SeaNymph's actual Harken parts, see [[furler]]: Harken 7311.21 1/2 (Unit 1 long link plate, ~$305) or 7410.20 series (Unit 0 toggle, ~$86)

## Contradictions / Surprises

- Research assumed Schaefer 1100/2100 series — this is a common furler for CD25D-sized boats but SeaNymph is confirmed Harken. Schaefer parts will not fit.
