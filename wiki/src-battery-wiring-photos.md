## Summary

Four photos documenting the as-installed battery compartment wiring on SeaNymph as of September 2025. Three wide-angle shots show the compartment layout from different angles; a fourth (taken May 2026) is a close-up of the Blue Sea SI-ACR label confirming the model.

The SI-ACR is confirmed physically installed — prior wiki pages described it as "planned." A blue Victron SmartShunt is also visible in the compartment (shunt bar wired inline on the negative circuit, consistent with SmartShunt wiring practice).

## Key Takeaways

- **Blue Sea 7610 SI-ACR is installed** — not planned. Confirmed from both the Sep 2025 compartment photos and the May 2026 label close-up.
- **SI-ACR model confirmed:** 7610, 12/24V DC, 120A DC, IP67, with Start Isolation, Dual Sensing.
- **Wiring color scheme observed on SeaNymph:**
  - Heavy red/pink cables — battery positive runs to ACR studs A and B
  - Yellow cables — likely solar input from Victron MPPT (enters the compartment)
  - Black cables — ground/negative
  - Black corrugated split loom — used for cable protection on longer runs
- **Victron SmartShunt visible** in lower-left of compartment — blue rectangular unit with a shunt bar wired inline on the negative circuit. The inline shunt wiring (conductors on both sides of the body) confirms this is a SmartShunt (not a BMV-712 head unit). Specific amperage rating (500A vs 1000A) not readable from photos.
- **Compartment construction:** plywood shelf visible; ACR mounted within the main battery compartment.
- Battery visible in upper portion of compartment.

## Contradictions / Surprises

- `battery-management.md` and `batteries.md` both described the SI-ACR as "planned" — it is in fact installed. Both pages updated.
