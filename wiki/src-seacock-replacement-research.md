# Cape Dory 25D Seacock Replacement Research

**Type:** AI research report (Gemini)
**File:** raw/Cape Dory 25D Seacock Replacement Research.md

## Summary

Comprehensive guide to seacock and backing plate replacement on the CD25D. Covers: the five standard seacocks and their sizes, the hull liner construction that makes port-side access extremely difficult, why plywood backing plates must be replaced, G10 as the gold standard material, hardware selection (flanged seacocks only — no in-line ball valves), and a step-by-step installation process. References Maine Sail / Rod Collins tutorials and Cape Dory Board forum threads as primary sources.

## Key Takeaways

- Standard CD25D has 5 below-waterline seacocks
- Original Spartan Marine bronze seacocks; high quality but aged
- Hull liner construction makes port cockpit scupper seacock extremely hard to access
- G10/FR-4 is the only recommended backing plate material; StarBoard is explicitly unsafe
- Use 3M 4200 or SikaFlex 291, never 3M 5200
- Maine Sail tutorials at pbase.com/mainecruising are the definitive reference
- Cape Dory Sailboat Owners Association (CDSOA) and capedory.org forums are key resources
