## Summary

Five files covering the full anchoring problem for a CD25D with no windlass. Together they address: anchor selection for a heavy-displacement 25-footer, sizing the Fortress aluminum anchor correctly, safe manual rode retrieval without windlass equipment, chain hook options for snubbers and retrieval rigs, and the technique for using the cockpit winch to haul chain.

## Key Takeaways

- Modern scoop anchor (Rocna/Mantus/Spade, ~22 lbs) is the right primary for the CD25D — faster set, better reset after shifts than traditional plough
- Combination rode: 25–30 ft of 1/4" G4 chain + 150–200 ft of 1/2" three-strand nylon; rope-to-chain splice preferred
- Fortress FX-7 (4 lbs) is undersized for the CD25D's 5,120 lb displacement — FX-11 (7 lbs) is minimum; FX-16 provides more margin
- **Fortress resetting failure is the defining weakness** — after a 180° wind or tide shift, the Fortress can break out and lie flat without re-anchoring; use the primary scoop for overnight stays with tidal reversals
- Power-set is essential: engine astern at ~1,000 RPM after deploying scope; watch bow for movement
- Weighing solo: motor toward anchor, haul slack, wait for vertical rode, cleat off, let buoyancy break anchor free — do not haul against a loaded rode
- Cockpit winch retrieval: chain hook + 1/2" line + snatch block on bow cleat → cockpit genoa winch; never run chain on winch drum
- Balsa-cored deck on CD25D is vulnerable to chafe from chain — use chafe mat
- Wichard Chain Grip (plate-style) is the recommended chain hook for retrieval; Mantus Chain Hook (locking gate) for snubbers
- Anchor alarm (GPS chartplotter or app) is the primary no-crew anchor watch tool

## Contradictions / Surprises

> **AI-generated.** The Fortress sizing question revealed a gap between manufacturer recommendations and heavy-displacement reality. Fortress's own chart recommends FX-7 for 16–27 ft boats. The CD25D's D/L ratio of 333.24 puts it in a different load category than a light 25-footer — Cape Dory forum data shows CD28/30/32 owners using FX-16 and FX-23. The files explicitly address this and recommend FX-11 as CD25D minimum. This is a case where manufacturer charts undersell what the boat actually needs.

> The Fortress resetting failure is not widely advertised by the manufacturer but appears consistently in independent test data and cruiser forum accounts. It is a real limitation, not an edge case. The wiki explicitly flags this.

> The chain hook sourcing file is the most narrowly focused of the five — it provides a useful product catalog but little new conceptual information. Wichard and Mantus are the specific brands worth knowing.
