# Maintenance Schedule — SeaNymph

Consolidated maintenance schedule for SeaNymph. Engine intervals are from the official Yanmar 1GM Service Manual (authoritative). Seasonal and haul-out tasks are based on standard coastal Maine practice.

> **Hours tracking:** The 1GM has no hour meter from the factory. Install one, or keep a running log of engine hours per trip. Without hour tracking, intervals default to time-based estimates.

---

## Before Every Start

1. Check engine oil level (dipstick: cylinder block, exhaust side)
2. Check clutch case oil level (dipstick: top of clutch case housing)
3. Check fuel level
4. After starting: confirm cooling water flowing from exhaust outlet
5. Confirm oil pressure warning lamp extinguishes after start
6. Check exhaust smoke — clears within moments
7. Visual check for oil, water, or fuel leaks
8. Check **weep hole** on raw water pump — a few drops is normal wear; a steady drip is imminent seal failure

## Engine — Hours-Based Intervals

All from Yanmar 1GM Service Manual. See [[engine]] for full detail.

### Break-In (new engine or post-rebuild)
- **20 hours:** First oil change (crankcase + clutch case)
- **30 hours:** Second oil change
- **50 hours:** Check/adjust V-belt tension, valve clearances, propeller shaft alignment; tighten cylinder head bolts (M10: 7.5 kg-m)

### Every 100 Hours
- Change engine oil — crankcase (1.3 L)
- Change clutch case oil (0.25 L)
- Clean fuel filter
- *Same oil for both — API CB/CC, SAE 20W or 30 for Maine*

### Every 250 Hours
- **Inspect raw water pump impeller** — check for wear, nicks, cracks
- Check/adjust V-belt tension (water pump: 5–7 mm; alternator: 10 mm at 10 kg force)
- Replace fuel filter element
- Clean intake silencer (air filter)
- Check/adjust propeller shaft alignment

### Every 300 Hours
- Replace oil filter element

### Every 500 Hours
- **Replace raw water pump impeller** (mandatory even if undamaged)
- Replace anticorrosion zinc
- Inspect mixing elbow for scale buildup
- Check/clean thermostat

### Every 1,000 Hours
- Check/adjust fuel nozzle spray pattern and injection timing *(dealer)*
- Tighten cylinder head bolts

### Every 4 Years (time-based, regardless of hours)
- Replace all rubber hoses
- Replace all V-belts

## Spring Commissioning

Do this before the first sail of the season.

**Engine:**
- [ ] Reinstall batteries (charged over winter)
- [ ] Open raw water intake seacock (was tagged CLOSED from winterization)
- [ ] Check engine oil and clutch case oil
- [ ] Check fuel level; inspect fuel filter
- [ ] Start engine; confirm cooling water at exhaust within 30 seconds
- [ ] Check all hose connections for winter cracking or loosening

**Rigging:**
- [ ] Inspect standing rigging — look for broken strands, cracked swage fittings, toggle wear, cotter pin condition
- [ ] Inspect running rigging — check halyards and sheets for chafe at turning points
- [ ] Confirm all blocks, clutches, and deck hardware turn freely; re-grease if stiff
- [ ] Check spreader tip boots/tape for chafe damage

**Deck and Hull:**
- [ ] Operate all seacocks — they should turn smoothly; apply Teflon grease if stiff; a seacock that won't move is a safety issue
- [ ] Check all through-hull hoses for cracking, softness, or deterioration
- [ ] Inspect anchor locker and ground tackle — check shackle pins, rode condition, chafe at roller

**Safety Gear:**
- [ ] Check flare expiration dates (USCG visual distress signals expire 42 months from manufacture)
- [ ] Inspect life jackets and harness for condition; test inflatable bladder
- [ ] Check fire extinguisher gauge and hydrostatic test date
- [ ] Test bilge pump (manual and electric)
- [ ] Confirm VHF radio works; verify MMSI is programmed

**Dinghy:**
- [ ] Inflate Zodiac; check for leaks and valve condition
- [ ] Apply 303 Aerospace Protectant to tubes
- [ ] Service Yamaha F2.5 — check oil, inspect fuel system, test start

## Fall Winterization

See [[winterizing]] for full procedures and checklist. Key engine items:

- [ ] Oil change (warm engine, API CB/CC, SAE 20W or 30) — crankcase + clutch case
- [ ] Raw water cooling system flushed with pink antifreeze
- [ ] Cylinders fogged
- [ ] Fuel treated (stabilizer + biocide) and engine run 15 min
- [ ] Raw water seacock closed and tagged
- [ ] Batteries removed, on maintainer
- [ ] Yamaha F2.5 carb run dry; outboard stored on its side
- [ ] Zodiac deflated, cleaned, 303 Protectant applied

## Annual — Haul-Out

Minimum annual tasks on the hard.

**Bottom:**
- [ ] Inspect bottom paint; apply antifouling as needed
- [ ] Inspect keel-to-hull joint for cracking or weeping (keel bolt corrosion indicator)
- [ ] Check keel bolts for rust weeping or looseness

**Through-Hulls and Seacocks:**
- [ ] Operate every seacock; note any that are stiff or won't turn fully
- [ ] Inspect backing plates (any softness = waterlogged core)
- [ ] Inspect through-hull hose connections from the outside

**Running Gear:**
- [ ] Inspect propeller for dings, barnacles, or damage; have repaired if needed
- [ ] Inspect propeller shaft seal (stuffing box or dripless seal); repack or replace if leaking excessively
- [ ] Inspect shaft zinc; replace if more than 50% consumed

**Hull Zinc:**
- [ ] Replace hull zinc(s) — replace at haul-out regardless of remaining material; cheap insurance

## Multi-Year Schedule

| Interval | Task |
|---|---|
| Every 2 years | Re-swing compass; update deviation table if any equipment changes near compass |
| Every 2 years | Professional rigging inspection (or any time a swage fitting shows cracking) |
| Every 4 years | Replace all engine rubber hoses and V-belts (Yanmar factory spec) |
| Every 4 years | Replace standing rigging if original — 30-year-old wire is end-of-life |
| As needed | Seacock replacement if stiff, corroded, or pad is waterlogged |
