# Rockland Harbor — Home Port Operations

Rockland is SeaNymph's home port. Working harbor with ferry service to Vinalhaven and North Haven (Maine State Ferry Service). Active commercial waterfront. Known as the "Lobster Capital of the World." Famously protected by a nearly mile-long granite breakwater extending from Jameson Point — but the harbor is large enough to generate its own internal seas from SW fetch and frequent ferry wakes.

## Harbor Character

- **Bottom:** Sticky mud — excellent holding for anchoring, well-suited for mushroom anchors and granite mooring blocks
- **Swell:** Predominantly from the south or southwest in summer (60–70% from south, 20–30% SW). South end of harbor most exposed. Behind the breakwater (Ocean Pursuits area) most protected from most directions.
- **Wakes:** Maine State Ferry Service runs to Vinalhaven and North Haven; ferries reportedly "don't slow down until well within the mooring field." Vessels moored near the ferry terminal experience frequent high-energy wakes. CD25D's full keel induces a rhythmic roll that stresses mooring pennants.
- **Noise:** Working harbor — ferry activity, events (Lobster Fest, Blues Fest). North end near Jameson Point is quietest.
- **Harbor Master:** Molly Eddy. City of Rockland Harbor Department: (207) 594-0312.

## Swell Exposure by Harbor Region

| Harbor Region | Swell Exposure | Wake Exposure | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| North end (Jameson Point area) | Low | Low | Sheltered by breakwater; quietest area |
| Central harbor | Moderate | Moderate | Some exposure to southerly fetch |
| South end / Public Landing | Moderate-High | High | Ferry lane proximity; significant wake |
| Behind breakwater (Ocean Pursuits) | Low | Very low | Best all-around protection |
| Knight Marine / ferry terminal | Moderate | Very High | Directly exposed to ferry wakes |

Implication for CD25D: **Ocean Pursuits moorings behind the breakwater are best shelter**. Knight Marine trades convenience for ferry wake exposure. The CD25D at 5,100 lbs will handle the chop but mooring pennants and chafe gear need to be sized appropriately.

### Mooring Regulations

- All moorings require bi-annual inspection by an approved diver (Fields Dive Service or Calligan Dive Service — Rockland-area inspectors)
- Bi-annual inspections are required by City of Rockland; annual inspections strongly recommended
- Rental moorings require City permit and Army Corps of Engineers permit
- Non-resident permit fee: $200/year for vessels under 30 feet; resident: $100/year
- Mooring permit requires proof of excise tax payment

### Seasonal Timing

- Contact marinas **January** to express interest — popular seasonal moorings fill by early February
- February–March: finalize rental agreement, provide insurance documentation
- April: pay City excise tax, apply for municipal mooring permit
- May 15: traditional season start; have mooring inspected before relying on it
- October 15: typical season end

## Knight Marine Service — Detailed Notes

- Location: 525 Main Street, adjacent to Maine State Ferry terminal
- Contacts: Celia Knight (manager), Dale and Laurie (yard staff)
- VHF monitoring: Channel 9 and 16
- Services: Travelift, chandlery, repair shops, showers, 24-hour laundry
- Labor: $105/hr general, $120/hr specialized
- Outside summer storage: $30/foot (June 21–Sept 19)
- Strong operational track record; hosts NYYC and Marblehead YC groups

**Knight Marine advantage:** If you need a parking space, want boatyard services immediately available, and value being across the street from Hamilton Marine, this is the most logistically efficient choice. Ferry wakes are the compromise.

## Dinghy Access

- Public Landing dinghy tie-up: first-come, first-served; fee or free for town mooring users
- Snow Marine Park: dinghy spots available
- Most marinas include dinghy tie-up with seasonal mooring

## Anchoring in Rockland

Anchoring possible but the harbor is heavily used. Sticky mud bottom provides good holding. Avoid the southwestern shoals (near Knight Marine) and northern shoals (near North End Shipyard) where depths can drop to 1 foot at mean low water.

## Paper Navigation Supplies

Hamilton Marine stocks Weems & Plath navigation tools including the standard triangles (#101 with handle ~$24–30, #103 ~$20–25). West Marine in Portland also stocks these.
