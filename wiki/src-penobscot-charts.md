# SeaNymph Chart References — Penobscot Bay and East

**Origin:** Edgar's personal chart bookmark list
**File:** raw/PenobscotCharts.md

## Summary

Edgar's reference list of NOAA charts covering the passage from Rockland east through Deer Isle Thorofare, Blue Hill Bay, and out to MDI / Southwest Harbor / Bar Harbor. Includes a note on the Maptech Waterproof Chartbook for Penobscot Bay as an alternative to individual chart sheets.

Charts are available print-on-demand from OceanGrafix (oceangrafix.com) — up-to-date NOAA prints on waterproof paper.

## Key Takeaways

- **13302:** Penobscot Bay and Approaches (home waters)
- **13315:** Deer Island Thorofare and Casco Passage
- **13316:** Blue Hill Bay
- **13312:** Frenchman and Blue Hill Bays and Approaches
- **13321:** Southwest Harbor and Approaches
- **Maptech Waterproof Chartbook: Penobscot Bay** — covers the home waters area in one volume

## Contradictions / Surprises

> No contradictions. This adds four charts beyond 13302, which was the only chart previously noted in the wiki.
