## Summary

Original Cape Dory Yachts factory brochure for the 25D. Contains a profile sail plan drawing, a top-down deck plan, a specifications table, and a small sailing photo. The sail plan clearly shows a full-keel sloop with a **conventional transom stern** — confirming the correction made 2026-04-29. The deck plan shows cockpit layout and cabin arrangement.
