## Key Takeaways

- **Model: Zodiac CFR 240** (Cadet Fastroller) — correction from prior "Cadet 240" label
- **HIN: XDCV2013A999** — XDC = Zodiac International; A9 = January 1999; 99 = model year 1999
- **Year: 1999** — boat is ~27 years old as of 2026
- **Material: PVC** — Zodiac "Strongan Duotex" thermo-welded PVC. Hypalon cannot be welded (must be glued); the fused seams on this boat confirm PVC
- **Sandpaper test**: lightly sand inconspicuous area — PVC stays same color; Hypalon turns suede-like/matte
- **MEK/acetone test**: PVC becomes tacky immediately; Hypalon is unaffected
- **UV degradation**: 27-year-old PVC is sensitive to UV degradation; signs are stickiness or brittleness. Use **303 Aerospace Protectant** regularly. Maine UV is lower than tropics but cumulative exposure is significant at this age.
