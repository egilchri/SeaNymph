# Bus Bar / Positive Cable Routing Photo — SeaNymph

**Type:** Photo (as-installed documentation)
**Author/Origin:** Edgar Gilchrist
**File:** raw/PXL_20250919_132001736.jpg

## Summary

One photo taken 2025-09-19 showing the cable routing area beneath the cabin sole or under-seat compartment — distinct from the battery compartment photos (taken 2025-09-24). Shows multiple heavy-gauge cables running through the bilge/interior area toward the battery compartment.

The photo confirms that **yellow is the primary positive cable color** used throughout SeaNymph's 12V DC system. Multiple yellow cables are visible running in parallel — these are the main positive distribution cables, not solar-specific wiring. A bus bar is likely just off-frame; the yellow cables originate from or terminate at it.

Also visible: black (negative) cables running alongside, a red and a pink positive cable (shorter or secondary runs), black corrugated split loom for cable protection, and what appears to be a bronze seacock with corrugated hose at lower right.

> **Note:** The bus bar itself is not clearly visible in this frame — it is presumably just off-frame in the direction the yellow cables converge. A follow-up photo centered on the bus bar would help complete the wiring documentation.

## Key Takeaways

- **Yellow = positive** is the consistent color code for main positive cables throughout SeaNymph's DC system
- **Black = negative** throughout
- **Red and pink** are also used for positive on some runs (shorter or secondary circuits)
- Multiple yellow cables run in parallel — consistent with a bus bar distribution point feeding several circuits
- The yellow cables seen in the battery compartment photos (entering from the left) originate from this routing area, not directly from the Victron MPPT
- Black corrugated split loom is used throughout for cable protection
- Bronze seacock with corrugated hose visible — consistent with the known below-waterline fittings in this area of the boat

## Contradictions / Surprises

- Prior note in `battery-management.md` misidentified the yellow cables in the battery compartment as "solar input from Victron MPPT." Corrected: yellow cables are the main positive distribution cables; solar input from the MPPT arrives via a separate run whose color is not yet confirmed.
