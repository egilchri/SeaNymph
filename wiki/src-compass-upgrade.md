# Cape Dory 25D Compass Upgrade Options — AI Research

**Origin:** AI research report on compass replacement and compensation for the CD25D
**File:** raw/Cape Dory Compass Upgrade Options.md

## Summary

A thorough analysis of steering and hand-bearing compass options for the Cape Dory 25D, with good CD25D-specific magnetic environment context. Covers why the diesel engine and electronics cause deviation, recommended replacement compasses (Ritchie BN-202, Plastimo Contest 101), hand-bearing compasses (Plastimo Iris 50, Vion Mini 2000), a DIY compensation procedure, and maintenance guidance.

The compensation procedure (cardinal heading 180° method with GPS COG as reference) is the most actionable new content — it shows how to reduce deviation rather than just living with it.

## Key Takeaways

- **CD25D magnetic sources:** Yanmar diesel (primary), bronze portlights, stainless chainplates; lead ballast does NOT contribute; electronics/wiring add EMI
- **Steering compass top picks:** Ritchie BN-202 (4.5" CombiDial, 5-yr warranty) for helmsman readability; Plastimo Contest 101 (dual cockpit+cabin read, built-in clinometer) for cruising versatility
- **BN-202 mounting hole:** 5.75 inches (146 mm)
- **Hand-bearing compass:** Plastimo Iris 50 (photoluminescent, no batteries, one-handed) — practical choice; Vion Mini 2000 (infinity optics, no parallax) — precision choice
- **DIY compensation:** cardinal heading procedure using GPS COG in slack tide as reference; half-error rule on each pass; residual deviation still needs to be recorded
- **Bubble in compass bowl = leak or diaphragm failure** — replace bowl assembly if significant
- **UV cover mandatory** when boat not in use; keep teak chemicals away from dome

## Contradictions / Surprises

> No contradictions with existing wiki content. The compensation procedure (DIY cardinal swing) is entirely new — the existing compass.md covered re-swinging professionally but not how to do it yourself.

> The Plastimo Contest 101's built-in clinometer is a non-obvious bonus for judging reefing decisions — worth noting for a singlehander.

> GPS COG as a reference for compass compensation is a modern convenience that makes DIY compensation practical where distant landmarks are unavailable — common in Maine fog.
