## Summary

Detailed guide to installing a Blue Sea A-Series 30A ELCI breaker on the CD25D. Covers ELCI function (30mA ground fault trip, electric shock drowning prevention), CD25D-specific considerations (limited panel space, old wiring, likely ground-neutral bonds), step-by-step wiring procedure, and common troubleshooting (nuisance tripping from pre-existing wiring faults).

## Key Takeaways

- ELCI is required by ABYC E-11 within 10 feet of shore power inlet
- CD25D AC panel is typically under the main cabin table or near the galley
- Old wiring on 40-year-old boats often has ground-neutral bonds that cause nuisance tripping
- Both hot and neutral pass through the toroidal coil; ground wire does NOT
- Test the ELCI monthly with the test button
- Blue Sea A-Series 30A ELCI (part 3102100) is the standard recommendation
