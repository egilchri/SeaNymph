## Key Takeaways

**Winterizing:**
- Oil change while engine is warm; API CD mineral oil ONLY on 1GM
- Bucket for antifreeze flush must be at pump height inside the boat, not on the ground (14 L/min pump flow; self-priming distance limited)
- Thermostat opens 42°C, fully open 52°C
- Fuel: fill to 95%, add stabilizer AND biocide, run 15 min
- Professional winterization documentation is now essential for Maine insurance claim defense

**Winter Cover:**
- Boom-tent style; raise boom 12–18 inches above sailing position with topping lift
- Tight downhauls prevent swing/chafe — critical
- Coated polyester (Top Gun/Odyssey) or polyester canvas: 8–12+ years
- Poly tarps: avoid; shred in winter; trap moisture
- Six vents minimum for 25-footer; non-breathable materials need deliberate air gaps
- Custom cover break-even vs. annual shrinkwrap: 2–4 years
- YKK #10 Vislon zipper for access door at companionway

**Mast Climbing:**
- Bosun's chair: ½" nylon braid, bowline, safety line advanced as you rise
- ATN Top Climber, SJS PrimeClimb: dedicated mast-climbing devices
- Rock climbing ascenders: most adaptable if you have climbing experience
- Helper mandatory on deck; never alone aloft
- Riggers charge several hundred dollars per climb — worth it for occasional work

**Tablet Mount:**
- Tiller sweep eliminates aft cockpit placement
- Companionway is "active zone" — avoid centerline mounted at hatch height
- Starboard bulkhead with articulated RAM arm is the recommended design
- King Starboard backing plate; 316 SS; nylock nuts; fender washers; 3M 4200
- Pot all holes in balsa core with epoxy before fastening
- RAM Mounts (lifetime warranty) vs Scanstrut ROKK Mini (mechanical locking)

**Engine — Raw Water Pump:**
- Yanmar 1GM raw water pump: part 128170-42200
- Pump positioned above ferrous oil pipe — weep hole drip → pipe rust → catastrophic oil loss
- Water seal: spring faces impeller; oil seal: spring faces bearings
- Quality impeller kit sources: Poseidon Marine (~$47), Parts4Engines (~$55)
- Bearing brands: KOYO, SKF, NTN
- Check for milky oil after any reassembly

**Insurance:**
- 2025 Maine hard market: Loss of Use removed; electrical breakdown excluded; freezing excluded; "seaworthy condition" clause (score 2/5 — most dangerous due to vagueness)
- Professional winterization documentation essential
- Insist on Agreed Value over ACV
- Local brokers: William E. Dennett Agency (Kittery), North Atlantic Insurance Associates

**Harken Furler Parts:**
- Unit 1 MKIV Long Link Plate: part 7311.21 1/2, ~$305.95 at Fisheries Supply (4 in stock)
- Unit 0 MKIV standard toggle: 7410.20 series, ~$85.95
- Unit 0 ESP leg kits: 7320.21, ~$366.90
- Hamilton Marine Rockland: 207-594-8181 to confirm local stock
- Must identify which unit is installed before ordering — Unit 0 and Unit 1 parts are not interchangeable

**Epoxy/Fiberglass Safety:**
- Sensitization is permanent and irreversible — the core risk
- OV/P100 cartridge mandatory: 3M 60921 or 60923 (not a dust mask)
- Nitrile gloves 6–8 mil; never latex or cotton
- ANSI Z87.1 D3/D4 chemical splash goggles
- Never use solvents on skin (increases epoxy penetration)
- Tyvek coveralls recommended

**Grommets:**
- Spur grommets (rolled rim + spur washer) = gold standard for marine canvas
- Plain grommets = inadequate; hardware store kits = unsuitable
- Nickel-plated brass for most work; stainless for permanent applications
- Hoover Pres-N-Snap + spur dies (~$250) = minimum adequate tool for DIY
- Sailrite HandyPress = better tool for volume work and stainless
- Webbing loops as no-grommet alternative for heavy covers

**Anchor Rode Marking (Zip Ties):**
- Mark every 25 feet: Blue/Green/Red/White/Yellow (1, 2, 3, 4, 5 marks)
- Thread ties through chain links (not around outside)
- 2–3 ties per mark for redundancy
- Trim tails flush
- Red warning cluster at last 15–20 feet of rode

**Snatch Blocks:**
- For CD25D anchor retrieval: SWL 1,500 lbs+ minimum
- Harken 75mm, Lewmar Synchro, Ronstan Series 60: all solid choices
- Garhauer 25-SB: stainless, "practically indestructible," best price-to-strength
- The Marine Exchange (Kittery, ME): good used hardware source

**Navigation Triangles:**
- Weems & Plath #101 (with handle): ~$24–30; #103 (without handle): ~$20–25
- Hamilton Marine Rockland stocks Weems & Plath tools — call ahead to confirm
- Defender Marine online is typically lowest price

**BBQ:**
- Measure stern rail diameter before buying any BBQ mount
- Magma kettle (A10-080 mount): fits 7/8" or 1" round rail
- Kuuma (budget, 58182 mount): fits 7/8"–1¼"
- Dickinson Marine: premium option

**CD25D Plans/Drawings:**
- Carl Alberg's archive (sketches, blueprints, models for all Alberg designs including Cape Dory) is at the Peabody Essex Museum, Phillips Library, Rowley MA
- Contact: research@pem.org or 978-542-1553
- Also: Cape Dory Sailboat Owners Association forum (capedory.org) for owner-shared drawings

**Temflex 2155 Application:**
- Stretch to half original width while wrapping (activates self-fusing)
- 50% overlap per pass
- Overwrap with 3M Super 33+ or 88 vinyl tape (Temflex has poor UV resistance)

## Contradictions / Surprises

> **AI-generated.** The insurance file's "seaworthy condition" clause analysis is the most important finding in the cluster — it's a vague catch-all that could be invoked to deny almost any claim. It scores 2/5 for reasonableness in the research, meaning it's seen as especially concerning. This is not widely discussed among DIY boat owners but could materially affect claim outcomes.

> The grommet research is surprisingly comprehensive — it reads like an engineering document rather than a simple how-to. The most useful finding is the tool compatibility issue: spur dies are brand-specific and cannot be reliably mixed. Sailrite is the cleanest vendor for die-grommet matching.

> The Yanmar water pump/oil pipe proximity issue is a CD25D-specific safety finding not commonly discussed in general Yanmar 1GM literature. The weep hole inspection interval should be much shorter than typical engine checks (monthly, not annually).

> Two winterizing files were too large to read fully (first 100 lines only). The content captured is consistent and covers the key procedures, but the full antifreeze flush procedure details and any CD25D-specific notes in the later portions of those files were not captured.
