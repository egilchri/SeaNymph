## Summary

Comprehensive research into the Victron SmartSolar MPPT 75/15 controller installation, Renogy panel cable modification for Victron screw terminals, panel mounting options (stern rail vs. companionway hatch), flexible vs. rigid panel comparison, and complete shopping list (~$400–700 hardware). Cape Dory Solar Panel Install file strongly discourages companionway hatch mounting due to foot traffic, shading, and heat buildup.

## Key Takeaways

- Victron 75/15 handles two 50W panels (verify total voltage/current within 75V/15A limits)
- Battery must be connected before panels — connect battery first, always
- Renogy cables need MC4 connectors removed and re-terminated for Victron screw terminals
- Companionway hatch is a bad mounting location — stern rail mount recommended
- Flexible panels have 2–5 year realistic lifespan in harsh marine use; rigid panels last 20+ years
- ETFE-coated flexible panels are more durable than PET-coated
- 10 AWG tinned marine wire appropriate for 15A system on 25ft boat
- Recommended fuse: 20–25A between controller and battery
