## Key Takeaways

- ST2000+ is correct choice for CD25D — ST1000 likely underpowered at CD25D's displacement
- 589mm is the critical tiller-pin-to-socket distance — measure on the actual boat before purchasing
- Mount socket on starboard coaming; reinforce with backing plate and through-bolts
- Dedicated 12V circuit, 14 AWG tinned wire, 3–5A fuse
- ST2000 is NOT waterproof — must protect or remove when not in use
- Two failure modes: water ingress (most common) and broken belt (sudden)
- 0.5–1.5A draw in auto mode — minimal impact on house bank
- ST2000 lacks stall detection; Simrad TP32 has it — ST2000 can self-damage in hard conditions
- Tiller pilot and wind vane are complementary tools, not competing
- Slop in any connection point causes hunting; eliminate before relying on pilot
