## Summary

Official Yanmar operation manual for the GM series marine engines (1GM10 with KM2P marine gear, 1GM10C with SD25 sail-drive, 1GM10V with KM3V marine gear). Covers product overview, component identification, oil specifications, daily checks, engine operation, periodic maintenance schedule and procedures, troubleshooting chart, long-term storage, principal specifications, and wiring diagrams. 94 pages; read targeted sections covering ~50 pages.

### Oil Specifications

**Engine oil:**
- API Service Category: **CC or higher** (TBN value 9 or more)
- Viscosity: **SAE 10W30 or 15W40** (both suitable year-round in normal temperatures)
- Never use API CG-4 or CH-4
- Never mix oil types or brands
- Capacity: 1.5 L (1.59 qt)

**Marine gear oil (KM2P):**
- API Service Category: CD or higher
- Viscosity: SAE #20 or #30
- Capacity: 0.3 L (0.32 qt)

### Maintenance Schedule (from official Yanmar table)

**Before every start (daily):**
- Visual: engine exterior, fuel level, oil level (engine + marine gear), battery charge warning lamp
- After starting: inspect seawater outlet flow, inspect for fuel/oil/water leaks, exhaust gas color

**After initial 50 hours:**
- Drain fuel tank and fuel filter/water separator
- Change engine oil + replace oil filter
- Change marine gear oil
- Check/adjust alternator V-belt tension
- Inspect/adjust intake-exhaust valve clearances *(dealer)*
- Inspect/adjust throttle and shift cables *(dealer)*
- Adjust propeller shaft alignment *(dealer)*

**Every 50 hours:**
- Drain fuel filter/water separator

**Every 150 hours:**
- Change engine oil
- Change marine gear oil
- Inspect battery electrolyte level (serviceable batteries only)

**Every 250 hours or 1 year (whichever first):**
- Drain fuel tank
- Replace fuel filter element
- Inspect fuel injection nozzle spray pattern *(dealer)*
- Replace engine oil filter element
- **Inspect seawater pump impeller**
- Inspect zinc anode (replace if < 50% original size)
- Clean intake silencer (air filter)
- Clean exhaust/water mixing elbow
- Clean breather pipe
- Check/adjust alternator V-belt tension
- Check wiring connectors
- Inspect/adjust intake-exhaust valve clearances *(dealer)*
- Inspect/adjust throttle and shift cables *(dealer)*

**Every 1000 hours or 4 years (whichever first):**
- Inspect fuel injection timing *(dealer)*
- **Replace seawater pump impeller** (mandatory even if not damaged)
- Check/adjust alternator V-belt tension
- Tighten all major nuts and bolts
- Adjust propeller shaft alignment *(dealer)*

### Key Maintenance Details

- **Alternator V-belt tension:** Correct tension = 8–10 mm deflection at mid-span between pulleys
- **Oil filter installation:** Hand-tighten until gasket seats, then 3/4 turn with wrench; torque 20–24 N·m (14–17 lb-ft)
- **Zinc anode:** Inside the anode plug, labeled "Anticorrosion Zinc." Inspect minimum every 300 hours; inspect more frequently in aggressive (more corrosive) water
- **Mixing elbow:** Replace every 500 hours or 2 years, whichever first, even if undamaged
- **Max cruising RPM:** 3400 rpm or less; operate at max RPM (3600) for no more than 5% of total engine time (30 min per 10 hrs)
- **After long non-use:** Before starting, open seacock and fuel cock, put in NEUTRAL, then crank to distribute oil before attempting to start. Running dry causes seizure.

### Long-Term Storage

1. Wipe engine exterior clean
2. Drain water from fuel filters
3. Drain fuel tank completely OR fill to prevent condensation
4. Grease exposed joints of remote control cables
5. Seal intake silencer and exhaust pipe to prevent moisture ingress
6. Drain bilge completely
7. Waterproof engine room
8. Charge battery once per month during storage
9. Remove key and cover key switch with moisture cap

**Draining the seawater cooling system:**
1. Put pan under seawater drain cocks
2. Loosen drain cock at thermostat cover, drain water from inside
3. Remove seawater pump side cover (3 bolts), drain pump body, reinstall cover

**Returning to service:**
1. Replace oil and oil filter before first run
2. Fill fuel, prime fuel system
3. Run at idle 1 minute
4. Check fluid levels and for leaks

## Contradictions / Surprises

**Critical corrections to existing [[engine]] wiki page:**

1. **Displacement:** Wiki said 232 cc — **manual says 318 cc (0.318 L).** The 232 cc figure is wrong; correct value is 318 cc.

2. **Cooling system:** Wiki said "freshwater (closed loop) with raw water heat exchanger." The official manual specification says **"Direct seawater cooling (Rubber impeller water pump)"** — seawater flows directly through the engine, there is no closed freshwater loop on the standard 1GM10. This is a significant architectural difference. The thermostat in the system regulates direct seawater flow, not a separate coolant circuit. Verify on the physical engine — if there is a heat exchanger mounted on the engine, it may be a non-standard installation; the factory spec is direct raw water.

3. **Oil specification:** Wiki said "API CD mineral-based, SAE 15W-40 **only**, no synthetics." Manual says **API CC or higher, SAE 10W30 or 15W40 are both acceptable.** The "CD-only, no synthetics" claim was from prior AI research, not the official manual.

4. **Oil capacity:** Wiki said ~1.3 L. Manual says **1.5 L (1.59 qt) total.**

5. **Output:** Wiki said ~9 HP at 3,200 RPM. Manual says **continuous 8 hp @ 3400 rpm; maximum 9.1 hp @ 3600 rpm.**

6. **Maintenance intervals:** Wiki had impeller "annually or every 2 years." Manual says **inspect every 250 hours; mandatory replacement every 1000 hours or 4 years.** Oil change: wiki said "annually or 100 hours" — manual says **every 150 hours.**
